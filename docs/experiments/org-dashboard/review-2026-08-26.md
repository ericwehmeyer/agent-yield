# Review of the org dashboard prototype, 2026-08-26

Reviewer: Opus, adversarial pass, against the two-level brief. Author: Fable.
Verdict **RETHINK** -- narrow, but a rethink and not a fix list.

Two foundational decisions have to be re-taken -- the numerator's unit and the
aggregate's form -- after which most of the page survives. Every number, axis
and claim re-derives from them, so it cannot be patched forward.

## Blocking

**B1. The headline is denominated in raw `usage.total`, which this repo has
already retired for scoring.** `dashboard.html` L371 `tokPerIns`, L297-302, the
hero tile L501, every axis; `design.md` L24-30. `thresholds.py` L76 says it in
the file the page was told to read: tokens, and CRUDE ONES, ~97% cache reads at
0.10x a base input token. Two teams with identical tok/ins can differ by a
factor in dollars on cache-hit profile and model mix -- and a team that restarts
sessions often, which is this repo's own recommended discipline, posts a lower
cache-read total for the same work and ranks better. A Director reassigns
headcount on a cache-hit ranking. `design.md` defends the DENOMINATOR as least
gameable; the numerator is the unexamined half.

**B2. The mix panel's central claim is arithmetically impossible for the metric
it is about.** L672-674. It prints, bolded, that Client Portal reads -23% on the
headline while its docs share moved 44% -> 57%: *cheaper lines, not cheaper
work*. `tokens_per_insertion` is `tokens / (code + docs + other)` and the
COMPOSITION of that denominator does not appear in the expression -- a pure mix
shift at a fixed line total leaves the any-ratio exactly unchanged. Run from the
page's own functions: tokens fell 2.5% while inserted lines rose 27%. The -23%
IS the line count. The panel that is the page's designated defence against #46
teaches a false mechanism. The correct tell is on the page and unpointed-at:
the triple goes 10,762/23,609/24,230 -> 8,249/25,057/14,453, the docs half 40%
"better" and the code half 6% worse while the whole moved for another reason.

**B3. The org hero tile is a pooled ratio with no team-weight guard -- the mix
artefact one level up, on the headline.** L479-482, rendered L501-502. Pooled
12,073 -> 12,176 (+1%); team weights held at prior, 12,073 -> 12,327 (+2.1%).
The tile understates the org's move 2.3x because the cheapest team wrote a
larger share of the lines (15.4% -> 18.6%). The mix panel decomposes by line
KIND, which B2 shows is harmless to this metric, and never by TEAM, which is the
composition that actually moves the aggregate. NEXT.md's own dashboard rule:
no decomposable aggregate appears without its decomposition beside it.

**B4. UNSCORABLE is substituted by zero in the thing that ranks.** L563
`(r.yCur || 0)` and L555's descending sort on a lower-is-better metric: a team
with no corpus in the window gets a 2px stub in LAST place, which on this axis
is the visually best team in the org. Verified by injecting a gap team: rank 8
of 8. Same class at L668, where `null > 0` is false so an unscorable delta
paints green. This fires the moment the falsifier's own success condition does
-- a second real leaf whose corpus starts mid-window.

**B5. Every caveat naming the real team is a hardcoded literal, so it cannot
track the data it caveats.** L584, L638, L508 ("1 real"), L631, L672. Two of the
figure headings assert magnitudes ("The divergence is eight weeks old", "One
improvement is a mix shift") as string literals that render whatever the data
says. The honesty layer is the layer that goes stale first, silently, in the
direction of understating how much invented data is on screen. Rendering a
one-child rollup printed *Payments is Infinityx the median of its siblings* and
*its five siblings hold flat* over one series.

**B6. "Its five siblings hold flat" is false on the chart it labels.** L632.
Weekly w7/w0: Payments 0.94x, Platform 0.99x, Trading Tools 1.07x, Market Data
0.90x, Client Portal **0.69x** -- the second-largest movement on the figure,
drawn in de-emphasis grey and called flat, and it is the same team the next
panel down singles out as the tell. Two adjacent figures say opposite things
about one series.

**B7. The real leaf's numerator and denominator were captured at different
instants, and the page carries no capture time.** L268-273. 2026-08-25
reproduces to the digit and the scope line reproduces exactly. 2026-08-26's
NUMERATOR reproduces to the token; its denominator does not -- page 50 commits
/ 3,208 code / 4,273 docs against the CLI's 51 / 3,213 / 4,293, and NEXT.md's
19:54 capture of the same command reads 49. Three denominators for one frozen
numerator. Sized honestly at 0.3%, not 25x, and blocking anyway: rule 1 is about
provenance, not magnitude, and the page asserts "Same work on both sides" while
giving the reader no timestamp with which to notice.

## Non-blocking

- **N1.** The rollup renderer is not recursive, so the two-level cut's own
  justification does not hold. `teamStats` reads `team.days`; an internal node
  has none and a three-level tree throws. Zero children throws; one child
  renders `Infinityx`. Implement `daysOf(node)`, or delete the claim.
- **N2.** Cost-band shares are stored bare while the LABEL is dynamic, which
  inverts `CostBandShare`'s pinning rule: after a retune the page prints stale
  shares under a correct-looking new legend.
- **N3.** L579's median joins the real leaf's 2-day figure into a 28-day
  comparison set. 11,849 with it, 11,627 without; both print "1.6x", so it does
  not bite today.
- **N4.** `design.md` names the IdP as the only input this repo cannot produce
  and lists reflog attribution as solved plumbing. Reflogs are per-clone, never
  pushed, and expire; collecting one from every developer's every clone before
  expiry is the harder sourcing problem.
- **N5.** The falsifier's escape clause is set on noise -- a 3.86x bar drawn
  from this leaf's own n=2 within-day spread. The delete trigger itself is good.
- **N6.** Drill-down works and is focusable; `go()` drops focus to `<body>`,
  `role="link"` on a `<tr>` breaks row semantics, the tooltip is a spammed live
  region, no view is linkable and Back exits the page.
- **N7.** Self-containment and theme-awareness pass in all three theme states.
  At 375px the 640-unit SVGs scale to 0.59x and 9.5px axis labels render at
  ~5.6px.
- **N8.** `CUR_LABEL`/`PRIOR_LABEL` computed and unused; `merges` carried and
  never rendered; `d === 0` classes as better.

## The author's three self-criticisms

1. Synthetic shapes smuggle in conclusions -- **UNDERSTATED**. The conclusions
   are not smuggled through the data; they are typed into the markup (B5), and
   one of the two derived sentences beside them is false (B6) while the other is
   impossible (B2). Changing the seed changes nothing.
2. THRESHOLDS frozen at build time -- **UNDERSTATED, and the direction is
   backwards**. Frozen-and-disagreeing is the safe failure. What is built is
   labels that re-render and shares that do not (N2).
3. The 2-day figure is visually rankable -- **CONFIRMED**, mis-sized. It lands
   3rd of 7, inside the pack where it reads as ordinary, and it is the one bar
   at full opacity while the other six are 0.55.

**The larger thing nearby.** All three self-criticisms are about scaffolding.
None is about the metric, whose numerator is the crude one (B1).
